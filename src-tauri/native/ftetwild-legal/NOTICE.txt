Ecky native fTetWild worker notices
===================================

Runtime: fTetWild commit d7d99bb4387a07895b9adce058dc7305f6b6e5ab
Adapter protocol: ecky-ftetwild-worker-v1

fTetWild and Ecky's modifications to its covered files are distributed under
Mozilla Public License 2.0. Corresponding source is shipped beside the binary.
The Ecky worker adapter source and the complete patch against the pinned
upstream revision are included in that archive.

The worker uses structured arrays. It does not invoke or link TetGen, Gmsh,
FreeCAD, Python, CalculiX, a network service, or an STL interchange path.
oneTBB is disabled; deterministic worker thread count is one.

Bundled dependency versions, source revisions, source URLs, and licenses are
listed in transitive-licenses.json. License texts are shipped under legal/.
GMP remains a separate dynamically linked runtime library.
